GREED PIT Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

GREED PIT is designed to work without an account, advertising profile, or analytics account. This policy describes the current release candidate of the GREED PIT mobile game.

Data collection

The current GREED PIT app does not transmit gameplay or personal data to the developer. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account registration, location access, contacts access, camera access, microphone access, or tracking across apps and websites.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, unlocked content, run statistics, active-run recovery data, and feedback drafts/reports are stored locally on your device so the game can remember your progress and preferences. This information remains on the device unless you choose to copy or share it using another app or service.

Feedback

The in-game feedback tool stores reports locally. Choosing to copy a report places that text on your device clipboard. GREED PIT does not automatically upload feedback to the developer.

Third-party software

The mobile app is packaged with Capacitor to run the game as an iOS application. The current build does not add advertising or analytics services. Apple and your device may process information according to Apple’s own policies when you download, install, or use apps from the App Store.

Children

GREED PIT does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the current app does not transmit user data to the developer, there is no developer-held account or profile data to delete.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored game information remains on the device until it is overwritten by normal gameplay, cleared by the app, or removed when the app’s stored data is deleted. Because the current release does not maintain developer-side user accounts or gameplay databases, there is no server-side user profile to request for deletion.

Changes

If a future GREED PIT version adds analytics, accounts, advertising, online services, or other data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Support and privacy questions

Use the GREED PIT support page for current support and contact options: GREED PIT Support.
