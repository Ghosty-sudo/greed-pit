## Description

GREED PIT is a fast, one-handed survival action game about pushing your luck without losing everything.

Move by dragging anywhere on the screen while your character attacks automatically. Defeat enemies, collect XP, stack upgrades, and survive increasingly hostile waves. Every run becomes its own strange build as your damage, attack speed, projectiles, movement, and other abilities grow.

GREED changes the rules. Trade a normal level-up for a GREED contract: immediate power with a permanent consequence. The deeper you go, the more dangerous the Pit becomes.

Every five minutes, make the decision that defines the run:

CASH OUT to bank your haul safely.

GO DEEPER to increase your reward multiplier and face a harder Pit.

Features:
- One-handed portrait gameplay
- Short sessions with long-run potential
- Stackable upgrades and build combinations
- GREED contracts with meaningful tradeoffs
- Bosses, elite enemies, hazards, and escalating cycles
- Persistent Scrap progression and unlockable Bad Ideas
- Unlockable character skins
- Music and sound effects with a persistent audio toggle
- Active-run recovery for interrupted mobile sessions
- No ads, no gacha, and no account required

How deep can you go before GREED collects the bill?

## Review notes draft

GREED PIT is a portrait-only survival action game. Drag anywhere on the gameplay canvas to move; attacks fire automatically. The first launch includes a three-panel tutorial that can be skipped. Every five minutes the player can Cash Out or Go Deeper. The title screen includes Audio, Feedback, and Privacy controls. Feedback reports are stored locally unless the user manually copies and shares them.

No login credentials are required. No ads or in-app purchases are present in the current binary.
